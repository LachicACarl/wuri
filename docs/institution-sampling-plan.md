# Institution sampling plan

## Scope boundary

The platform may serve multiple Philippine HEIs, but the dissertation's
empirical sample is a separate research decision. The platform population
must not be mistaken for participating institutions or a completed sample.

## Working coverage decision

**Researcher decision recorded 2026-10-16:** use a mixed design.

- The platform population remains broad and may support all Philippine higher
  education institutions that can be represented safely.
- The empirical A0–A4 evaluation will use a selected sample of eligible
  institutions for which authorized, usable evidence is obtained.
- Platform coverage is therefore not a claim that every Philippine HEI will
  participate or contribute research data.

This is a working design decision, not formal protocol approval. The selected
sample, sampling frame, eligibility rules, sample-size rationale, and
recruitment outcomes remain unresolved.

**Leading proposal recorded 2026-10-16:** stratified purposive sampling for
adviser review. This remains a proposal, not an approved sampling method.

The proposal covers Philippine HEIs only, considers public and private
institutions and regional representation, evaluates eligibility separately
for each of WURI 2026, THE World University Rankings 2026, QS World
University Rankings 2026, and UI GreenMetric 2025, and includes only
institutions that meet approved criteria and provide authorized, usable
evidence. Final strata and sample size remain unresolved until the CHED frame,
research objectives, recruitment feasibility, and analysis requirements are
reviewed.

## Proposed nested sampling structure

For adviser review, the current proposal is:

- institution as the primary recruitment and sampling unit;
- campuses as nested operational units when a framework or source
  distinguishes them;
- institution-framework assessment as an eligibility and evidence unit; and
- WURI innovation programs/cases as nested program units, not institution
  substitutes.

This structure is documented as a proposal only. The final primary unit,
campus treatment, institution-framework eligibility, and WURI case
relationship require researcher/adviser approval. See
[`sampling-design-options.md`](./sampling-design-options.md) for alternatives.

## Decisions requiring researcher approval

The following are intentionally not selected by the software:

- target population version and inclusion date;
- unit of sampling (institution, campus, or institution group);
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- probability, stratified, purposive, or census sampling;
- regional, sector, institution-type, and framework-eligibility strata;
- target sample size and power/precision rationale;
- treatment of multi-campus institutions;
- non-response and replacement rules;
- whether one institutional corpus may support more than one framework;
- authorization, DUA, privacy, and ethics requirements.

For the mixed design, the researcher must additionally approve:

- whether the selected sample is probability-based, purposive, stratified,
  or another design;
- the population from which the sample is selected;
- the framework-eligibility strata and minimum representation, if any;
- the target sample size and its precision, feasibility, or power rationale;
- how institutions that decline, withdraw, or cannot provide complete
  authorized evidence are handled;
- whether replacement institutions are permitted and how replacement bias is
  reported.

The sample must be selected only after the CHED manifest is frozen and the
researcher has approved the sampling frame and eligibility rules. No
institution is currently represented as a participating research subject by
this repository.

## Sample-size and recruitment boundary

No institution sample size is selected. The dissertation's 70:15:15 split
applies to labeled evaluation cases, not institutions. The dissertation
states at least 30 purposively selected system evaluators for software-quality
evaluation, which is a separate respondent group and must not be counted as
participating HEIs.

Institution recruitment, non-response, withdrawal, incomplete evidence,
replacement, and selection-bias reporting remain unresolved and require
researcher/adviser and, where applicable, ethics approval.

## Platform versus sample

Institution records and memberships are platform entities. Authorized
documents, passages, assessments, datasets, expert reviews, thresholds,
experiments, and predictions must remain scoped to their institution.
Shared framework definitions and versioned catalogs are not institution-owned.

## Safeguard

The 120-record WURI abstract pilot is a private candidate program corpus. It
is not a Philippine HEI sample, institutional evidence, or a basis for
estimating population coverage.

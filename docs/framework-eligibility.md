# Framework eligibility and unit of assessment

The dissertation editions remain fixed: WURI 2026, THE World University
Rankings 2026, QS World University Rankings 2026, and UI GreenMetric 2025.
Eligibility is framework-specific and cannot be inferred from a CHED listing
or an institution name.

| Framework | Unit of assessment | Required inputs indicated by methodology | Eligibility status |
|---|---|---|---|
| WURI 2026 | Innovative program/case in a category, not automatically an institution-wide ranking | Program description, implementation, outcomes/impact, costs/benefits, and supporting exhibits under the applicable WURI template | Institution/program eligibility rules and complete numerical scoring rules require source verification. |
| THE 2026 | Institution | Institutional submissions, teaching and research data, reputation inputs, bibliometrics, international and industry measures; edition-specific provider eligibility rules apply | Unknown for any HEI until the provider's 2026 eligibility conditions and required data are checked. |
| QS 2026 | Institution | Reputation surveys, bibliometric measures, employability/outcomes, faculty/student, international, and sustainability data | Unknown for any HEI until provider eligibility and data availability are checked. |
| UI GreenMetric 2025 | Institution/campus sustainability submission as defined by the guideline | Campus setting/infrastructure, energy, waste, water, transportation, education/research measures and indicator-level submissions | Unknown until the complete 2025 guideline and institution/campus submission requirements are verified. |

## Interpretation boundary

An institutional readiness assessment is an operational study construct. It
does not reproduce an official provider ranking unless all provider-required
data, normalization, eligibility, and submission conditions are available.
WURI program readiness must not be presented as an institution-wide WURI
ranking score.

The proposed catalog in
[framework-criteria-catalog.json](./framework-criteria-catalog.json) records
methodology inputs and verification status; it does not decide eligibility
for a named institution or authorize evidence use.

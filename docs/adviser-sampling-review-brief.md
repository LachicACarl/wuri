# Adviser review brief — Philippine HEI sampling proposal

## Purpose and status

This brief summarizes the proposed sampling design for adviser review. It
does not approve the sampling method, sampling unit, strata, sample size, or
research protocol.

The platform and the dissertation study have different scopes:

- The platform may support a broad population of Philippine higher education
  institutions (HEIs).
- The empirical A0–A4 evaluation will use a selected group of eligible
  institutions that provide authorized, usable evidence.

No institution has been enrolled as a research participant, and no
institutional evidence was collected for this proposal.

## Dissertation-supported facts

Chapter 3 explicitly describes purposive selection for specialized
respondents. It specifies a panel of five domain experts and at least 30
purposively selected system evaluators, with an approximate practical
composition of 20 institutional users and 10 IT/software evaluators
(Sections 3.3.1–3.3.3 and Table 3.2). These are respondent groups, not an
institution sample size.

Chapter 3 also specifies a 70:15:15 train/validation/test allocation for
labeled evaluation cases, stratified by framework and major indicator group
where sample size permits (Sections 3.5.4 and 3.7.4). This is a case-level
dataset split, not a rule for the number of participating HEIs.

The available dissertation text does not specify a national HEI sampling
frame, institution-versus-campus primary unit, number of participating HEIs,
or probability-based precision/power calculation for the institutional
evidence corpus. The relevant chapter headings place Section 3.2 at printed
page 37 and Section 3.3 at printed page 40; exact page placement of the
Section 3.3.3 paragraph should be confirmed against the author's paginated
copy.

## Leading proposal: stratified purposive sampling

The leading proposal is **stratified purposive sampling**, retained as an
unapproved option for adviser review.

The proposal would:

1. Freeze and reconcile a dated candidate population frame.
2. Define meaningful strata only after checking which fields are complete and
   reliable in that frame.
3. Invite and purposively recruit institutions that meet approved criteria
   and can provide authorized, usable evidence.
4. Report recruitment, non-response, withdrawal, incomplete evidence,
   replacements if allowed, and selection limitations.

This design fits the practical requirement for institution-authorized
evidence and is compatible with the dissertation's purposive respondent
language. However, the dissertation does not explicitly state that
institutional evidence units must use stratified purposive sampling. Adviser
confirmation is required.

## Population and scope

The proposed population is Philippine HEIs, excluding elementary and
secondary schools. Public and private HEIs may be included, subject to
verified eligibility and evidence availability.

Candidate population sources currently documented are CHED Statistics, CHED
HEIDA, and the legacy CHED HEI list. The source review records a reported
2,334 HEIs as of June 2025, with coverage limitations including BARMM-related
exclusions. This is not treated as a definitive population count.

Before selection, the researcher must create an immutable manifest containing
source URL and retrieval time, reporting period, CHED identifier where
available, normalized name, sector/type, region, campus hierarchy, duplicate
decisions, missing-field flags, and coverage limitations. No such frozen
manifest currently exists.

## Proposed sampling and analysis units

The following nested structure is proposed, not approved:

- **Institution:** primary recruitment and proposed sampling unit.
- **Campus:** nested unit when a framework or source distinguishes main and
  satellite campuses.
- **Institution-framework assessment:** institution-by-framework eligibility,
  evidence, and assessment unit. Eligibility is assessed separately for WURI
  2026, THE World University Rankings 2026, QS World University Rankings
  2026, and UI GreenMetric 2025.
- **WURI program/case:** nested program-level unit for WURI submissions. It
  must not be treated as an institution, campus, or institution-wide ranking
  observation.
- **Evidence passage/document:** source and annotation units below the
  institution-framework assessment.

The analysis unit for each outcome must be stated separately. Institutions,
campuses, institution-framework assessments, documents, passages, WURI
programs, expert ratings, and system evaluators cannot be substituted for one
another.

## Eligibility and evidence access

Proposed eligibility requirements for adviser review include:

- inclusion in the approved Philippine HEI population frame;
- public or private status recorded from an authoritative source;
- framework-specific eligibility verified separately for each edition;
- clear institution/campus identity and parent relationship where relevant;
- institutional participation and document-provision authorization;
- permission for the proposed research use;
- sufficient provenance and usable evidence for the approved criteria;
- applicable privacy, ethics, DUA, source-rights, retention, and deletion
  determinations.

An institution that cannot provide authorized evidence must not be assigned
fabricated evidence, a zero readiness value, or an assumed negative label.
The approved protocol must define whether it is reported as non-participation,
withdrawal, or an incomplete eligible case.

## Possible strata

Regional representation should be considered, but final strata must wait for
the reconciled frame. Candidate dimensions for review include:

- Philippine region;
- public/private or other authoritative sector;
- institution type;
- framework eligibility;
- campus structure, if analytically relevant.

Strata must be based on frame information available before recruitment. They
must not be created from participation outcomes, and their minimum
representation or allocation must be justified. Framework eligibility may
require framework-specific strata or separate eligible subsets rather than a
single universal stratum.

## Recruitment and non-response

The eventual protocol should specify an invitation and information process,
institutional authorization, research-use permission, framework screening,
evidence completeness screening, withdrawal, document retention, and
deletion procedures.

Non-response and inability to provide complete evidence may introduce
selection and coverage bias. If replacement institutions are permitted, the
replacement pool and rule must be defined before recruitment, and original
selection, refusal, replacement, and potential bias must be reported. If
replacement is not permitted, the final eligible and participating counts
must be reported without silently changing the target.

No automatic institution enrollment or bulk document collection is permitted.

## Why sample size remains unresolved

No defensible institution count can be entered until the researcher specifies:

- the primary research estimand and intended generalization;
- the accessible, eligible population after frame reconciliation;
- the selected framework scope and minimum framework coverage;
- whether the analysis is institution-level, institution-framework-level, or
  another approved unit;
- the expected participation and non-response rate;
- the planned comparative, agreement, error, or association analyses;
- any precision, power, feasibility, or minimum-case rationale;
- the number of independent cases needed for expert labels and the 70:15:15
  case split.

The five experts, at least 30 system evaluators, and 70:15:15 case split do
not determine the number of participating HEIs. An institution sample size
must not be invented from any of those values.

## Bias and generalizability

Stratified purposive sampling is non-probability sampling. Representation
targets may improve transparency and diversity but do not create known
selection probabilities or automatic statistical generalization to all
Philippine HEIs. Access, authorization, framework eligibility, evidence
quality, region, sector, and willingness to participate may all affect
inclusion.

The dissertation should state the selected-sample inference boundary,
describe the accessible population, report recruitment and non-response, and
avoid presenting findings as nationally representative unless a separately
approved design supports that claim.
